Methods

Roofs are soft washed with low pressure rather than blasted, which is why the method is used on shingle, tile and metal roofs.

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and suits hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks. Soft washing uses low pressure plus cleaning solution for roofs, stucco, siding and screens.

Concrete is cleaned with a surface cleaner, a tool built to give flatwork an even finish.
