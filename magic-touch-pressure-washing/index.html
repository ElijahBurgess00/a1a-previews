FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Will pressure washing damage my roof?

Not the way we do it. Roofs are never blasted with high pressure. We use a low-pressure soft wash that kills algae, mold and lichen at the root and is safe for shingle, tile and metal roofs.

What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water and is ideal for hard surfaces like concrete driveways and sidewalks. Soft washing uses low pressure plus cleaning solutions to safely clean roofs, stucco, siding and screens.

Is it safe for my plants, pets and pool?

Yes. We take care to protect your landscaping and sensitive areas, and we ask that pets stay inside while we work. We'll walk you through everything before we start.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. As long as we can reach an outdoor water spigot and the areas being cleaned, we can take care of everything. Just let us know when you book.

How often should I clean my home's exterior?

In Central Florida's humidity, most homeowners wash their house and concrete once a year and soft wash their roof every 1 to 3 years, depending on shade and buildup.

How much does it cost?
